## The one-sentence version

You learn here by doing short missions with a team of good-guy hackers, looking things up in these lessons whenever you want, and asking for hints as often as you like.

It's like joining a team on a real case: short jobs, a mentor who answers questions, and nobody keeping score.

## Why it matters in security

Real security work is mostly being stuck, then working out the next small step. Professionals look things up all day. Getting comfortable with that is as useful as any command.

And because this is <Term id="ethical-hacking">ethical hacking</Term>, every mission shows you both sides: how a weak spot is found, and how it's fixed.

## How it actually works

- **Missions** are short story episodes, 5 to 20 minutes long. Each one starts with a briefing and a signed letter saying what your team may test, then gives you small steps, one at a time.
- **Hints** come in three for each step: a nudge, then the idea, then nearly the answer. Open as many as you need. Your mentor, Noor, is the face of those hints in the story.
- **Lessons** like this one explain one idea at a time. Marked words open their meaning when you hover over, tap or focus them, and the glossary has them all.
- **Mistakes are safe.** Every terminal has a Reset machine button that puts everything back.
- **Nothing is saved.** There's no account and no score. Every mission is open from the start, and you can replay any of them to find the secrets you missed.

## In practice

Your first mission, [Welcome to the team](/missions/intro-01), takes about 8 minutes. A short tour points at where to type, and your first step ticks within a minute.

## Common misconceptions

- **"Using hints means I'm not good at this."** Hints are how everyone learns here. Nobody sees how many you used, including you.
- **"I have to finish the lessons before the missions."** Start wherever you like. Missions explain what they need as you go, and link here for more.
- **"If I close the tab, I'll lose my progress."** There's no progress to lose. A mission is short enough for one sitting, and you can start any of them again whenever you want.
